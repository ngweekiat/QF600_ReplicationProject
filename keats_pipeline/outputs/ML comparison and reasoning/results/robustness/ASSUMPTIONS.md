# Robustness assumptions and interpretation

All input portfolios are saved results; no model was fit, retuned or reoptimised by these scripts. Dates are return months from the common sample supplied by the comparison runner. Monetary figures are USD.

* Paper SR_net uses gross return standard deviation. Utility uses sample gross-return variance with ddof=1. Annualisation is mean/cost/utility times 12 and volatility times sqrt(12). Turnover is the full sum of absolute trades.
* Cost scenarios multiply saved costs at fixed weights. Gamma scenarios change the evaluation penalty only. Hypothetical AUM scenarios multiply costs by new anchor wealth / saved anchor wealth; quadratic dollar impact divided by wealth scales linearly at fixed weights. They are not optimised frontier points.
* net_excess_leq_minus_one_months flags net excess return <= -100%. Algebraic means, variance, utility, and Sharpe are retained without trimming. This invalidates a synthetic compounded excess index; funded insolvency must be checked using total returns including the risk-free return and is separately reported by the audit.
* Saved unconstrained leverage and local-listing liquidity can produce extreme market-impact costs. No new leverage/participation constraint, listing consolidation, currency correction, or winsorisation is imposed. A large cost calculated correctly from saved inputs remains an economic investability limitation.
* Saved gamma/AUM point files are independently read and required to cover all common test months. Active capacity uses its separately calibrated gamma_TE; Utility there is evaluated at gamma=10 for total returns, not the benchmark-relative optimisation objective. Baseline grid Utility is evaluated at its optimised gamma.
* Size and liquidity thirds use actual me and dolvol within current saved membership each month, with average percentile ties. me is in source USD millions; dolvol is USD dollar volume. Ranked dolvol_126d is not treated as dollars. Exiting stocks are labelled outside_current_membership. Contributions sum to a portfolio; they are not independent tradable subgroup portfolios. No missing group observation is silently relabelled low liquidity.
* Construction HHI/top-10 use absolute weights divided by gross leverage; signed exposure preserves long/short direction. December formation turnover is a reconstitution-season diagnostic, not a causal attribution. Industry labels are saved ff12.
* Rank IC is Spearman correlation across stock predictions and saved next-month excess returns. Observed-only targets exclude explicit zero imputations. Broad and held populations are separate; only one-month forecasts exist. Theme association IC uses a simple mean of existing ranked features; it is neither fitted-model importance nor causal evidence.
* saved_input_catalog.csv independently discovers existing panel-plus-prediction inputs. input_only_no_saved_portfolio rows use the configured test calendar only for forecast and saved return-model hyperparameter diagnostics; they do not enter portfolio statistics, performance rankings, cost scenarios, or portfolio significance tests. Saved membership is an intended universe, not proof of actual portfolio holdings.
* Saved feature importance is utility loss after within-month theme permutation, not pure predictive accuracy; Markowitz uses a separate gamma=100, zero-wealth/no-TC construction and evaluates importance utility at gamma=10; it is not the main saved Markowitz-ML portfolio. PF-ML uses its net-utility policy, so magnitudes across models are not directly comparable. Permutation seeds assess that diagnostic's Monte Carlo error, not model training stability.
* Hyperparameter switch fractions include all saved annual choices, including pretest choices. Distinct strategy picks that share the same return model are repeated with the dataset identifier for traceability. Fixed Static-ML uses (k,u,g)=(1,1,0), has no annually selected portfolio hyperparameters, and inherits its validation-path positions rather than the tuned Static-ML* test-window initialisation. Its rule is saved separately.
