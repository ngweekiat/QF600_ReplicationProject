Current selection: US high-cap only, 2005–2014. These 50 were ranked using observed correlations, coverage, stability and persistence, with group quotas and redundancy penalties. No ML model was fitted for this selection. Old characteristic archives and their fitted results have been removed. Performance for the new 50 requires a later explicit refit.
